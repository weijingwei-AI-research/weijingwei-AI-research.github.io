Research Overview

Research focuses on medical imaging AI, particularly the development of AI foundation models and intelligent analysis methods for the precision diagnosis and treatment of cancer and other major diseases. Key applications include early disease screening, precise diagnosis, treatment response prediction, and prognostic risk assessment.
